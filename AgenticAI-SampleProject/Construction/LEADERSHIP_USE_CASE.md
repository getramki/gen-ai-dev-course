## Demonstration Scenario

### Multi-Agent Procurement Simulation

**Scenario**: $100K Multi-Category Procurement (Construction Example)

**Universal Agent Roles**
1. **Purchase Agent** (Buying Organization)
   - Budget optimization within allocated limits
   - Quality requirements and delivery constraints
   - Approval workflows and policy compliance checks
   - Multi-supplier comparison and selection

2. **Sales Agent A** (Supplier Company - Cement)
   - Profit maximization within corporate pricing policies
   - Inventory management and delivery optimization
   - Competitive positioning and market analysis

3. **Sales Agent B** (Supplier Company - Steel)
   - Margin optimization with volume-based pricing
   - Market-based pricing within company constraints
   - Supply chain coordination and capacity planning

**Applicable Across Industries**
- **Manufacturing**: Raw materials procurement (metals, chemicals, components)
- **Healthcare**: Medical supplies sourcing (equipment, pharmaceuticals, consumables)
- **Technology**: IT procurement (hardware, software, cloud services)
- **Retail**: Inventory sourcing (products, packaging, logistics)

**Negotiation Process**
1. **RFQ Broadcast**: Purchase agent creates detailed requirements
2. **Supplier Discovery**: Automatic identification of qualified vendors
3. **Multi-Round Negotiation**: 4-5 cycles of competitive bidding
4. **Optimization**: Each agent maximizes objectives within constraints
5. **Auto-Finalization**: Best offers selected and contracts generated

**Expected Outcomes**
- 15-20% cost savings compared to manual negotiation
- 90% reduction in procurement cycle time
- 100% compliance with company policies
- Complete audit trail and documentation
